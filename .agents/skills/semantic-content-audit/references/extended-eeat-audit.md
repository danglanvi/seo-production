# Extended E-E-A-T Audit (16 Points)

## Experience Audit (8 Points) — CRITICAL

Experience is the foundational differentiator. Audit experience signals with highest scrutiny.

### Scoring

| Item | Points | Check | Detection Method |
|------|--------|-------|------------------|
| First-hand usage evidence | 3 | Personal use demonstrated | Look for "I tested", "After X days of use", specific observations |
| Original evidence media | 3 | Photos/videos from actual experience | Check image metadata, original vs stock |
| Specific observations | 2 | Details beyond specs/official info | Unique findings not in product descriptions |

### Experience Signal Audit Checklist

**Strong Experience Signals (✓)**:
- Specific timeframes: "After 30 days of daily use..."
- Personal observations: "What I noticed that the specs don't mention..."
- Test methodology: "I tested this by..."
- Comparison from use: "Compared to [X] which I also used..."
- Unexpected findings: "Surprisingly, I found that..."
- Usage conditions: "In tropical humidity of 80%..."

**Weak/Missing Experience Signals (✗)**:
- Generic statements: "Users report that..."
- Spec recitation: "The product features..."
- Hypothetical language: "This could be useful for..."
- Third-party claims: "According to reviews..."
- No timeframe: "The battery is good" (vs "After 2 weeks, battery retained 95%")

### Experience Audit Questions

For each major section:

| Question | Yes | No | Severity if No |
|----------|-----|----|-----------------| 
| Is there evidence of actual use/testing? | | | Critical |
| Are specific observations included? | | | High |
| Is timeframe/duration mentioned? | | | Medium |
| Are original photos/evidence present? | | | High |
| Are test conditions specified? | | | Medium |
| Are unexpected/unique findings shared? | | | Low |

## Expertise Audit (3 Points)

| Item | Points | Check | Detection Method |
|------|--------|-------|------------------|
| Technical accuracy | 2 | No factual errors | Verify claims against authoritative sources |
| Domain language | 1 | Appropriate terminology | Check for correct use of technical terms |

## Authoritativeness Audit (2 Points)

| Item | Points | Check | Detection Method |
|------|--------|-------|------------------|
| Author credentials | 1 | Byline with relevant background | Check author page, credentials stated |
| Source quality | 1 | Reputable citations | Verify cited sources are authoritative |

## Trustworthiness Audit (3 Points)

| Item | Points | Check | Detection Method |
|------|--------|-------|------------------|
| No contradictions | 1 | Internal consistency | Check claims don't conflict |
| Transparent limitations | 1 | Caveats disclosed | Limitations acknowledged where relevant |
| Contact/accountability | 1 | Author reachable | Contact info, author page available |

## E-E-A-T Issue Examples

### Issue: Missing First-Hand Experience

**Detection**:
```
"The iPhone 15 Pro Max has excellent battery life and camera quality." ✗
```

**Fix**:
```
"After using the iPhone 15 Pro Max for 45 days, I recorded average screen-on time of 8.2 hours. The camera produced sharp images in low light at 10 lux, though I noticed 2-second shutter lag in Night mode." ✓
```

### Issue: Generic "Best" Claims

**Detection**:
```
"The Sony WH-1000XM5 is the best noise-cancelling headphone." ✗
```

**Fix**:
```
"After testing the Sony WH-1000XM5, Bose QC45, and Apple AirPods Max in identical conditions—a 65dB office environment and 85dB aircraft cabin—the Sony reduced ambient noise by 28dB compared to 24dB (Bose) and 26dB (Apple)." ✓
```

### Issue: Spec Recitation Without Observation

**Detection**:
```
"The laptop weighs 1.4kg and has 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD." ✗
```

**Fix**:
```
"At 1.4kg, the laptop felt comfortable in my backpack during daily commutes over 3 weeks. The 16GB RAM handled my typical workload—15 Chrome tabs, Slack, and VS Code—without slowdown." ✓
```

## SECTION A: CERTAINTY & PRECISION (Rules 1-8)

### Rule 1: Be Certain
**Convey information with precision and confidence. Avoid opinion-based words.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "This strategy should boost traffic" | "This strategy boosts traffic by targeting relevant keywords" |
| "Water may improve hydration" | "Water improves hydration" |
| "It could potentially help" | "It helps because..." |

**Modality Guide:**
- **Present tense**: Facts with consensus → "Water boosts immunity"
- **Can/could/may**: Research-based → "Studies suggest X may help"
- **Should**: Personal suggestion → "You should drink 8 glasses"

---

### Rule 2: Cut the Fluff
**Remove unnecessary words that don't add value.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "It is an undeniable fact that in order to achieve success, one must work hard" | "Hard work is essential for success" |
| "Basically, there are some ways to..." | "5 ways to..." |
| "In terms of the fact that..." | [Delete entirely] |

**Words to Cut:**
- basically, essentially, actually, really, very, quite
- in order to → to
- due to the fact that → because
- at this point in time → now

---

### Rule 3: Use Numeric Values
**Content without numbers doesn't reflect expertise.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "Our product has many features" | "Our product offers 7 key features, including real-time analytics" |
| "There are several ways" | "There are 5 ways" |
| "Most users prefer" | "78% of users prefer" |

---

### Rule 4: Qualify Instances
**Add descriptive details that specify and distinguish.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "The disease has symptoms" | "The disease presents with 3 common symptoms: fever, cough, and fatigue" |
| "There are some risks" | "There are 5 severe risks, including..." |
| "Several techniques exist" | "5 techniques for advanced users include..." |

**Qualifiers to Add:**
- Number (5 symptoms)
- Severity (severe, mild, common)
- Type (advanced, beginner)
- Context (for children, for professionals)

---

### Rule 5: Understand Context of Verbs
**Different verbs signal different contexts.**

| Context | Appropriate Verbs |
|---------|-------------------|
| Health/Wellness | boosts, improves, enhances, supports, strengthens |
| Skills | develops, builds, sharpens, hones, cultivates |
| Problems | causes, triggers, leads to, results in |
| Solutions | fixes, solves, addresses, resolves, eliminates |

**Example:**
- Health: "Vitamin C **boosts** immunity"
- Skills: "Practice **develops** proficiency"
- Problems: "Stress **triggers** headaches"

---

### Rule 6: Use Precise Numbers (YMYL Critical)
**For YMYL content, avoid approximations.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "About 5mg of active ingredient" | "5.25mg of active ingredient per dose" |
| "Around 3.7 liters" | "3.785 liters" |
| "Approximately $500" | "$497" |

---

### Rule 7: Provide Multiple Measurement Units
**Use dual units, prioritize target audience's preference.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "8 glasses" | "8 glasses (approximately 2 liters)" |
| "70 lbs" | "70 lbs (31.75 kg)" |
| "100°F" | "100°F (37.8°C)" |

**Priority:**
- Vietnam audience: metric first
- US audience: imperial first
- Include both when possible

---

### Rule 8: Be Detailed Yet Concise with Specifics
**Specify rather than generalize.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "The offer is valid for a short time" | "The offer is valid from May 15th to May 20th" |
| "Available in the morning" | "Available 5:00 AM - 9:00 AM" |
| "Recently updated" | "Updated March 15, 2024" |

## SECTION B: EXAMPLES & STRUCTURE (Rules 9-16)

### Rule 9: Give Examples After Plural Nouns
**Always provide at least 3 examples.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "Various marketing tools" | "Various marketing tools, such as Google Analytics, SEMrush, and Ahrefs" |
| "Many cryptocurrencies" | "Over 40 cryptocurrencies, including Ethereum, Bitcoin, and Litecoin" |

---

### Rule 10: Use Same Part of Speech in Lists
**Maintain parallel structure.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "Planning, to execute, and reviewing" | "Plan, execute, and review" |
| "Ensuring safety, clear communication, spend wisely" | "Ensure safety, clear communication, spend wisely" |

**Pattern:** All verbs, OR all nouns, OR all adjectives

---

### Rule 11: Optimize Subordinate Text (First Sentence After Heading)
**The first sentence must directly address the heading's topic.**

| Heading | ❌ Wrong First Sentence | ✅ Correct First Sentence |
|---------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| "How to do X" | "X is a common technique..." | "To do X, follow these 5 steps..." |
| "Benefits of Y" | "In modern times, Y has become..." | "Y offers 7 key benefits including..." |
| "What is Z?" | "Many people wonder about..." | "Z is [direct definition]..." |

**Match Format:**
- "How to" → "To [verb]..."
- "What is" → "[Term] is..."
- "Benefits of" → "[Topic] offers..."
- "Why" → "Because..." or "[Topic] [verb]..."

---

### Rule 12: Put "If" Statements Second
**Declaration first, then condition.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "If you want to improve, practice daily" | "Practice daily if you want to improve" |
| "If A becomes B, do X" | "Do X if A becomes B" |

**Pattern:** [Main statement], if [condition].

---

### Rule 13: Match Anchor Text with Target Page Title
**Anchor text should closely reflect the linked page's title.**

| Target Page Title | ❌ Wrong Anchor | ✅ Correct Anchor |
|-------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| "10 Ways to Improve Sleep Efficiency" | "click here" | "improve sleep efficiency" |
| "Advanced SEO Techniques for 2025" | "this article" | "Advanced SEO Techniques for 2025" |

---

### Rule 14: Use "You/Your" Over "We/Our"
**Address the reader directly.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "We believe these strategies are effective" | "You will find these strategies effective for your goals" |
| "Our team recommends" | "I recommend" or "The best approach for you is" |

---

### Rule 15: Ensure Logical Content Flow
**Maintain continuous connection between sections.**

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Introduction | States problem/question |
| Body | Addresses in logical order |
| Conclusion | Connects back to introduction |
| Headings | Flow from general → specific |

---

### Rule 16: Use Simple Present for General Truths
**Facts use present tense.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "Semantic SEO will focus on meaning" | "Semantic SEO focuses on meaning and context" |
| "Water has been essential" | "Water is essential for life" |

---

## SECTION C: ANSWER STRUCTURE (Rules 17-24)

### Rule 17: Don't Delay the Answer
**Answer immediately, then expand.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "In history, vision boards... [long background]... benefits include..." | "There are 5 main benefits of vision boards for entrepreneurs: [list immediately]" |
| "Many experts have studied..." | "The answer is X. Research by Y supports this..." |

---

### Rule 18: For "How-To", Don't Start with Definition
**Skip definitions in how-to content.**

| Article Type | ❌ Wrong Opening | ✅ Correct Opening |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| "How to Bake a Cake" | "What is a Cake? A cake is..." | "To bake a cake, start by preheating to 350°F..." |
| "How to do SEO" | "SEO stands for..." | "Follow these 7 steps to optimize your site..." |

---

### Rule 19: Bold the Answer, Not Keywords
**Emphasize the answer/insight, not search terms.**

| Question | ❌ Wrong Bold | ✅ Correct Bold |
|----------|--------------|----------------|
| "What is a penguin?" | "A **penguin** is a bird" | "A penguin is a **flightless sea bird**" |
| "What causes headaches?" | "**Headaches** are caused by..." | "Headaches are caused by **dehydration, stress, and poor sleep**" |

---

### Rule 20: Answer Questions in Matching Modality
**Match the question's verb form.**

| Question Type | Answer Modality |
|---------------|-----------------|
| "Does X help?" | "Yes, X helps..." (not "X is...") |
| "Can X cause Y?" | "X can cause Y..." (not "X causes...") |
| "Should I do X?" | "You should do X if..." |

---

### Rule 21: Declare Before Citing
**State the fact first, then cite source.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "According to Harvard, exercise helps..." | "Exercise reduces heart disease risk by 30%. A Harvard study (2023) confirms..." |
| "Research shows that..." | "X improves Y. Research by Z supports this finding." |

---

### Rule 22: Use Colons to Introduce Lists/Explanations
**A colon signals: what follows completes the thought.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "The phases are planning, execution, review" | "The project has three key phases: planning, execution, and review" |

---

### Rule 23: Maintain Word Order in Q&A
**Keep parallel structure between question and answer.**

| Question | ❌ Wrong Answer | ✅ Correct Answer |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| "What are the primary advantages of solar power?" | "Solar power is renewable, cost-effective, and eco-friendly" | "The primary advantages of solar power are renewability, cost-effectiveness, and low environmental impact" |

---

### Rule 24: Don't Start Paragraphs with Quotes
**Introduce quotes within sentences.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "'The user always comes first.' This principle guides design." | "Experts emphasize user experience, with one stating, 'The user always comes first.' This principle guides design." |

## SECTION D: FORMATTING & STYLE (Rules 25-32)

### Rule 25: Include 3+ Items in Examples
**When listing examples, provide at least 3.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "Tools like Google Analytics" | "Tools like Google Analytics, SEMrush, and Ahrefs" |
| "Countries such as Germany" | "Countries such as Germany, France, and Italy" |

---

### Rule 26: Use "and/or" Not Slashes in Headings
**Connect entities with words, not symbols.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "SEO/SEM Strategies" | "SEO and SEM Strategies" |
| "Beginner/Advanced Tips" | "Tips for Beginners and Advanced Users" |

---

### Rule 27: Explain Localized Terms
**Provide context for regional/cultural terms.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| Title: "Best Bánh Mì Shops" | Title: "Best Bánh Mì Shops" + Description: "Bánh Mì, a popular Vietnamese sandwich with savory meats, pickled vegetables, and fresh herbs..." |

---

### Rule 28: Proofread Thoroughly
**Check all content for errors before publishing.**

- [ ] Spelling errors
- [ ] Grammar mistakes
- [ ] Punctuation consistency
- [ ] Formatting uniformity
- [ ] Link functionality

---

### Rule 29: Create Semantic Image Alt Text
**Alt text must be descriptive and contextual.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "dog playing" | "Golden retriever catching a red ball in a sunny park" |
| "chart" | "Bar chart showing 40% increase in website traffic from January to March 2024" |

---

### Rule 30: Differentiate Facts from Possibilities
**Use present tense for facts; modal verbs for possibilities.**

| Type | Verb Usage | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| Established fact | Present tense | "The Earth is the third planet from the Sun" |
| Possibility | May/might/could | "These changes may lead to improved performance" |
| Suggestion | Should | "You should consider these factors" |

---

### Rule 31: Capitalize First Letter of Each Word in Headings
**Use Title Case for headings.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "What is search engine optimization?" | "What Is Search Engine Optimization?" |
| "Benefits of drinking water" | "Benefits of Drinking Water" |

---

### Rule 32: Format Paragraphs for Readability
**Keep paragraphs short with adequate spacing.**

- 2-3 lines per paragraph
- Clear visual break between paragraphs
- Consistent font style and size
- Adequate white space

## SECTION E: ADVANCED SEMANTIC RULES (Rules 33-40)

### Rule 33: Proper Word Sequence
**Place key attributes and context early in sentences.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "A car, which is red and fast, sped by" | "A fast, red car sped by" |
| "The technique, often used by experts, improves results" | "This expert technique improves results" |

**Why:** Search engines weight early words more heavily. Front-load important terms.

---

### Rule 34: Use Oxford Comma
**Include comma before "and/or" in lists of 3+.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "Research, writing and editing" | "Research, writing, and editing" |
| "Red, blue and green" | "Red, blue, and green" |

---

### Rule 35: Avoid Contradictions Within Article/Site
**Maintain consistency across all content.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| Article 1: "X is beneficial" + Article 2: "X is harmful" | Clear contextual differentiation OR consistent stance |
| Intro: "5 benefits" + Body: "4 benefits discussed" | Numbers match throughout |

---

### Rule 36: Avoid Fallacies
**No logical errors in arguments.**

Common fallacies to avoid:
- False cause (correlation ≠ causation)
- Overgeneralization ("all users prefer...")
- Appeal to authority without evidence
- Straw man arguments

---

### Rule 37: Use Active Voice
**Active voice is clearer and more direct.**

| ❌ Passive | ✅ Active |
|-----------|----------|
| "The report was written by the team" | "The team wrote the report" |
| "Results were analyzed" | "We analyzed the results" |

---

### Rule 38: Use Simple Bullet Points
**Avoid complex numbering systems.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| 1.1, 1.1.1, 1.1.2 | • Item 1 |
| Nested sub-numbers | • Item 2 |
| | • Item 3 |

---

### Rule 39: Avoid Personal Opinions in Facts
**Present information objectively.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "I feel like users are more engaged" | "Data shows 20% increase in user engagement" |
| "I think this works" | "Testing shows this increases conversion by 15%" |

---

### Rule 40: Use Positive/Neutral Language
**Choose words with appropriate connotations.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "This approach doesn't waste resources" | "This approach optimizes resource allocation" |
| "Not as bad as competitors" | "Better than competitors in X area" |

**Exception:** Use negative language only for warnings or adverse impacts.

## SECTION F: SEMANTIC OPTIMIZATION (Rules 41-45)

### Rule 41: Use Relevant Entities and Semantic Words
**Expert vocabulary demonstrates topical authority.**

| ❌ Generic | ✅ Expert/Semantic |
|-----------|-------------------|
| "Good phone" | "Flagship smartphone with advanced computational photography capabilities" |
| "Nice features" | "AI-powered noise cancellation with adaptive EQ" |

---

### Rule 42: Distribute Semantic Terms Properly
**Central entity throughout; micro terms in specific sections only.**

| Term Type | Distribution |
|-----------|--------------|
| Central Entity (e.g., "water") | Every section |
| Macro Terms (e.g., "hydration") | Multiple sections |
| Micro Terms (e.g., "constipation") | Only relevant section |

---

### Rule 43: Create Contextual Connections
**Justify internal links with surrounding context.**

**Pattern:**
1. Mention anchor text terms 1-5 times before linking
2. Create semantic bridge to linked content
3. Ensure logical flow to internal link

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "Click here to learn more" | "Understanding hydration is key to better health. Learn more about daily water intake requirements." |

---

### Rule 44: Match Active/Passive to Query Intent
**Different voice answers different queries.**

| Voice | Answers Query About |
|-------|---------------------|
| Active: "Financial advisors help families achieve freedom" | Financial advisors |
| Passive: "Financial freedom is achieved with advisor help" | Financial freedom |

---

### Rule 45: Complete Grammatical Sentences
**Every sentence must have subject + verb.**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "Cat sleeps" (incomplete) | "The cat sleeps" |
| "Because important" | "This matters because it improves results" |

>

It's the sentence around it."* → số liệu phải nằm TRONG câu S-V-O hoàn chỉnh quotable (đúng Rule 46 + 49), không rắc số rời. ⚠ COI vendor, correlational, trọng số = rubric nội bộ Surfer; refresh theo lint quý. Wiki: [[E-A-V Framework]] §Semantic Triplets. 🔒 Lớp phát hành (29/08/2026): 3 con số % này chỉ dùng **nội bộ** (blacklist register) — bản `-ca`/public chỉ giữ kết luận định tính "số phải nằm trong câu quotable".

### Rule 46: E-A-V Completeness
**Mỗi factual statement phải đủ Entity + Attribute + Value. Test: "Ai/cái gì (E) làm gì (A) với kết quả bao nhiêu (V)?"**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| "Vitamin C is important" | "Vitamin C supports immune function by increasing white blood cell production by 20%" |
| "SEO helps businesses" | "SEO increases organic traffic by 150% average in 6 months" |

### Rule 47: Strong Attribute Selection
**Attribute = predicate của câu. Thay verb yếu (is/are/has) bằng verb chỉ rõ action/impact.**

| Yếu | Mạnh thay thế |
|---|---|
| is / are | defines, represents, comprises, functions as |
| has | contains, provides, delivers |
| is important | improves, reduces, increases, enables |
| is good for | supports, strengthens, optimizes |

### Rule 48: Attribute Type Matching
**Chọn đúng LOẠI attribute theo loại statement — dùng sai loại làm NLP parse lệch intent:**

| Đang viết về | Loại Attribute | Verbs |
|---|---|---|
| Benefits/Harms | **Causal** | improves, causes, prevents, leads to |
| Definition | **Hierarchical** | is a type of, belongs to, falls under |
| Cách hoạt động | **Functional** | supports, enables, facilitates |
| Metrics/KPIs | **Measurement** | measures, indicates, quantifies |

❌ "Semantic SEO improves rankings" khi đang ĐỊNH NGHĨA (đó là benefit) → ✅ "Semantic SEO is an optimization approach that focuses on meaning and context".

### Rule 49: Value Precision by Entity Type
**Độ chính xác của Value theo loại entity: YMYL/medical = exact ("2 viên", "73.2% theo nghiên cứu X"); lifestyle chung = approximation chấp nhận được. Exact numbers = trust + Featured Snippet extraction.**

### Rule 50: Entity Definition at First Mention
**Entity mới (acronym, technical term, concept ít phổ biến) phải được định nghĩa ngay lần mention đầu — không định nghĩa = knowledge gap = tăng Cost of Retrieval.** (Phối hợp Definition Distribution Rule của outline: intro đã định nghĩa thì H2 không lặp.)

### Rule 51: Attribute Prominence Prioritization
**Test: "Định nghĩa entity mà THIẾU attribute này được không?" Không thể → High Prominence → vị trí sớm + coverage sâu.**

### Rule 52: Incremental Heading Order Preservation
**H1 dạng numbered ("5 Benefits: Muscle Growth, Recovery...") → các H2 phải theo ĐÚNG thứ tự đã nêu trong H1.**
⚠️ *Scope note:* rule này áp cho **numbered-H1 listicle**; với bài thường, thứ tự H2 theo RPP/Contextual Flow (skill outline Step 5) — không conflict.

### Rule 53: Sibling Heading Connection
**Các H2 cùng cấp phải có quan hệ semantic theo 1 trong 4 pattern: Sequential / Categorical / Comparative / Causal — không phải random collection.**

### Rule 54: Last Heading Intent Alignment
**Heading cuối align với chặng cuối của intent progression: Informational → takeaway; Commercial → recommendation/decision; Transactional → action.**

### Rule 55: Cross-Article Value Consistency
**Cùng E-A-V triple xuất hiện ở nhiều bài trong SCN → Value phải IDENTICAL. Số liệu vênh giữa các bài = untrustworthy signal (Google cross-reference Values).**

### Rule 56: Phrase Consistency Within Article
**Cùng concept = cùng terminology xuyên suốt bài (không "Semantic SEO" đoạn 1 rồi "SEO ngữ nghĩa" đoạn 3). Synonym rotation áp cho từ thường, KHÔNG áp cho tên concept/entity.**

### Rule 57: Query Cluster Coverage
**Bài target cả cluster query (cùng intent, khác phrasing + queries liên quan hay search cùng nhau) — thiếu 1 query trong cluster = gap topical coverage. Đối chiếu Query Network từ outline (3 Query Columns).**

### Rule 58: Question Canonicalization
**Nhiều cách hỏi cùng 1 concept → chọn 1 câu hỏi canonical làm heading, các biến thể answer trong đoạn — không mở heading riêng cho từng biến thể.** (Sentence-level của QDH/QDS gate.)

### Rule 59: Semantic Distance-Based Internal Linking
**Internal link theo semantic distance (search behavior + co-occurrence + shared attributes), KHÔNG theo cùng category. Hai topic khác category có thể rất gần về semantic.**

### Rule 60: Knowledge Domain Expansion Path
**Không jump thẳng giữa 2 knowledge domain xa nhau — đi qua intermediate domains để tạo semantic bridge ("Protein Powder" → "Workout Nutrition" → "Recovery" → "Sports Psychology").**

## QUICK REFERENCE CHECKLIST

### Before Writing
- [ ] Identify central entity and attributes
- [ ] Plan heading structure (H1→H4)
- [ ] Determine answer format per section

### During Writing
- [ ] L0 (Answer-First) for every H2/H3
- [ ] 3+ examples after plural nouns
- [ ] Numeric values, not vague quantities
- [ ] Subordinate text matches heading format
- [ ] Declaration before citation
- [ ] Active voice preferred

### After Writing
- [ ] No contradictions within article
- [ ] Parallel structure in all lists
- [ ] Oxford comma consistently used
- [ ] All headings in Title Case
- [ ] Facts in present tense
- [ ] Possibilities with modal verbs

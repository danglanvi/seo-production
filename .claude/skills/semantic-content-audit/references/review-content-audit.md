## Review Issue Examples

### Issue: Missing Drawbacks

**Detection**: Search for "however", "drawback", "issue", "problem"—none found.

**Fix**: Add honest drawbacks section:
```
"Despite strong performance, I encountered two issues: (1) The fan noise at 45dB during heavy loads became distracting in quiet rooms, and (2) The 65W charger runs noticeably warm after 30 minutes of fast charging."
```

### Issue: Single-Source Recommendation

**Detection**: Only one purchase link (affiliate suspected).

**Fix**: Add multiple sellers:
```
"Where to buy: Available at [Amazon], [Lazada], [Shopee], and [official brand store]. Prices ranged from [X] to [Y] during my research."
```

### Issue: "Best" Without Evidence

**Detection**:
```
"This is the best laptop for students." ✗
```

**Fix**:
```
"For students prioritising portability and battery life, this laptop outperformed the [3 alternatives I tested] with 12 hours of note-taking vs 8-9 hours average, and 1.2kg vs 1.4-1.6kg." ✓
```

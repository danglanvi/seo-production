# OKR GTV & TAG Framework

## Core Definitions

**OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)** = Strategic framework for driving organizational change. OKRs are "destination coordinates" (where you want to go), while KPIs are "dashboard metrics" (current status).

**Objective (O)** = Answers "What do we want to achieve?" Requirements: qualitative, inspiring, actionable, time-bound (usually quarterly), links to higher-level objectives.

**Formula:** `[Action verb] + [What to achieve] + [Specific number if applicable]`

**Key Results (KR)** = Answers "How do we know we achieved the Objective?" Must be SMART: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.

**Critical distinction - Outcome vs Output:**
| Output (Avoid) | Outcome (Prefer) |
|----------------|------------------|
| Viết 50 bài blog | Tăng traffic từ 10k lên 25k/tháng |
| Gọi 100 cuộc tư vấn | Ký được 5 hợp đồng Enterprise |
| Tham gia 10 sự kiện | Tăng brand mentions từ 20 lên 50/tháng |

**GTV Exception:** KR can be specific tasks if they directly contribute to Objective and are verifiable (done/not done). This is acceptable for "System & Process" focused years where building foundations matters more than pure outcomes.

## Cascading OKRs: Company → Department

**Critical Rule:** KR của công ty = O của phòng ban (hoặc 2 KR công ty gộp thành 1 O phòng ban)

**Structure:**
- Company: 3-5 Objectives, mỗi O có 3-5 KRs
- Department: 4-5 Objectives (cascade từ Company KRs), mỗi O có 4-5 KRs
- Individual: 2-3 Objectives, mỗi O có 3-4 KRs

**Cascade Example:**
```
Company O1 KR1 (Bộ công cụ bán hàng) ─┐
                                      ├→ Dept O1: Hoàn thiện công cụ & tài liệu
Company O1 KR2 (Tài liệu đào tạo) ────┘

Company O1 KR5 (Doanh thu ký mới) ────→ Dept O5: Chinh phục doanh thu Q1
```

**Link Tracking:** Mỗi O phòng ban phải ghi rõ "Link OKRs Công ty: O_ KR_" để tracking alignment.

## OKRs vs KPIs Comparison

| Criteria | OKRs | KPIs |
|----------|------|------|
| Purpose | Drive strategic change | Measure current performance |
| Focus | Outcome (change) | Output (activity) |
| Timeline | Quarterly, flexible | Year-round, continuous |
| Ambition | 70-80% achievement = good | 100% = standard |
| Structure | Always linked to Objective | Independent metrics |
| Setting | Top-down + Bottom-up | Usually manager-set |

**Relationship:** KPIs can become KRs (one-way). OKRs cannot be KPIs.

## Scoring System

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 0.0-0.3 | Không đạt | Almost no progress |
| 0.4-0.6 | Đạt một phần | Progress but insufficient |
| 0.7-0.8 | Đạt tốt | Target zone for stretch goals |
| 0.9-1.0 | Đạt xuất sắc | Exceeded expectations (may indicate target too easy) |

**Objective Score = Average of all KR scores**

## GTV SEO Philosophy 2026

**Năm Reboot - Tập trung System & Process**

2026 là năm GTV SEO tập trung xây dựng nền tảng hệ thống và quy trình, với mindset "process drives results". Doanh thu vẫn là KR quan trọng nhưng đóng vai trò lagging indicator - nếu hệ thống tốt thì kết quả sẽ đến.

**Core Principles:**
1. **Process over Outcome:** Tập trung xây hệ thống, doanh thu là lagging indicator
2. **Cross-functional collaboration:** Các O liên kết chặt chẽ với nhau
3. **Flexible tasks for PSP:** O3 PSP không có tasks chi tiết vì do SEO Lead Project đề xuất dựa trên dự án cụ thể
4. **Baseline tracking:** Luôn có baseline để đo lường tiến độ (VD: tỷ lệ win 22% → 27%, CPL 250k → 180k)
5. **Video đi kèm quy trình:** Mỗi quy trình mới phải có video đào tạo đi kèm
6. **HR owns OKRs tracking:** HR chịu trách nhiệm follow chấm OKRs toàn công ty hàng tháng

## SEO Project OKRs Structure

SEO projects typically span 10-12 months with quarterly OKRs following 3-level structure:

1. **Objective:** Strategic direction (3-4 per quarter)
2. **Key Results:** Measurable indicators (3-4 per Objective)  
3. **Tasks:** Detailed work items with deadlines and owners

### Four Pillars for SEO Project OKRs

**Technical:** Critical errors (5xx, 4xx), Core Web Vitals, Index Coverage, Page Load Time

**Content:** Number of articles, Traffic targets, Ranking positions (Top 10, Top 30), CTR

**Onpage:** Internal links, Page Load Time, Time on Site, Bounce Rate

**Offsite:** Backlinks (quantity + DR quality), Brand mentions, Social profiles, Domain Rating

### SEO Project Phases

| Phase | Quarter | Focus |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Foundation | Q1 | Strategy docs, Topical Map, Technical audit, Entity setup |
| Growth | Q2 | Content velocity, Traffic growth, Onpage optimization |
| Optimization | Q3 | Ranking maintenance, CRO, Advanced offsite |
| Sustainability | Q4 | KPI delivery, Handover, Upsale preparation |

## Tracking Cadence

**Company/Department:**
| Frequency | Duration | Content |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| Bi-weekly | 15-30 min | Confidence check 1-10, blockers |
| Monthly | 1-2 hours | Full scoring, cross-function alignment (HR organizes) |
| Quarterly | Half day | Company review, next quarter planning |

**SEO Project:**
| Frequency | Duration | Content |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| Weekly | 30 min | Traffic/ranking check, task progress |
| Monthly | 1-2 hours | Full OKRs scoring, Monthly Report |
| Quarterly | 2 hours | Strategy review, contract discussion |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Too many OKRs:** Limit to 2-4 Objectives, 3-5 KRs each
2. **KRs too easy:** Consistently scoring 1.0 means targets are too low
3. **No regular check-ins:** Must have bi-weekly (30min) and monthly (1-2hr) reviews
4. **Tying OKRs to compensation:** Keep separate from bonuses to encourage stretch goals
5. **Confusing tasks with KRs:** Always ask "So what?" to find the outcome
6. **Not linking dept OKRs to company:** Always specify "Link OKRs Công ty: O_ KR_"
7. **Ignoring baseline:** Always establish baseline before setting targets

## Quick Validation Questions

Before finalizing any OKRs, verify:
1. Is this Objective inspiring and actionable?
2. Does each KR have a specific number and deadline?
3. Are KRs measuring outcomes, not just outputs?
4. Is there a clear owner for each KR?
5. Do these OKRs link to higher-level objectives?
6. Is there a baseline measurement established?
7. Are there too many OKRs? (Max 4 Os, 5 KRs each)
8. For dept OKRs: Is "Link OKRs Công ty" specified?

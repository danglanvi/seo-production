# Client Discovery Questions for Topical Map Creation

## Pre-Engagement Questions

### Business Understanding

| Question (EN) | Question (VN) | Purpose |
|---------------|---------------|---------|
| What do you offer as a business? | Bạn cung cấp sản phẩm/dịch vụ gì? | Identify products/services |
| What is your website mainly about? | Website của bạn chủ yếu về chủ đề gì? | Identify Central Entity |
| What is the main purpose of your website? | Mục đích chính của website là gì? | Clarify goals |
| How do you plan to monetize? | Bạn dự định kiếm tiền từ traffic như thế nào? | Identify Source Context |

### Target Audience Understanding

| Question (EN) | Question (VN) | Purpose |
|---------------|---------------|---------|
| Who is your target audience? | Đối tượng khách hàng mục tiêu là ai? | Define personas |
| What is their main goal on your website? | Mục tiêu chính của họ trên website là gì? | Identify intent |
| What challenges do customers face before seeking solutions? | Những thách thức họ gặp phải trước khi tìm giải pháp? | Understand pain points |

### Competition Understanding

| Question (EN) | Question (VN) | Purpose |
|---------------|---------------|---------|
| Who are your biggest online competitors? | Đối thủ cạnh tranh online lớn nhất là ai? | Competitive analysis |
| What makes your brand different? | Điều gì làm brand của bạn khác biệt? | USP identification |

---

## Source Context Validation Questions

1. **Why are you covering this topic?** / Tại sao bạn phủ chủ đề này?
   - *Look for*: Clear business justification, not just "traffic chasing"

2. **How do you monetize?** / Bạn kiếm tiền như thế nào?
   - *Look for*: Direct product/service sales, affiliate, ads, lead generation

3. **Why should you exist in the SERP?** / Tại sao bạn xứng đáng tồn tại trong SERP?
   - *Look for*: Unique expertise, proprietary data, first-hand experience

## Buyer Persona Framework

### Demographics
- Age range, Gender distribution, Income level
- Education level, Occupation/industry, Geographic location

### Psychographics
- Lifestyle, Values, Interests and hobbies, Attitudes toward product category

### Behavioral
- How they find solutions, Typical buying journey
- Preferred content formats, Interaction with competitors

## Follow-Up Questions

| If... | Ask... |
|-------|--------|
| Source Context unclear | "What would happen to your business if you stopped creating content?" |
| Central Entity ambiguous | "If you could only rank for ONE topic forever, what would it be?" |
| Core vs Outer unclear | "Which topics directly lead to sales/conversions?" |
| Monetization undefined | "Show me your highest converting page. What is it about?" |

---

## Red Flags to Watch

| Red Flag | Implication | Action |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| "We want to rank for everything" | No Source Context clarity | Refocus on business model |
| "Traffic for ads" only | Weak SERP justification | May lose rankings over time |
| Multiple unrelated Central Entities | Site needs multiple Topical Maps | Discuss scope/priority |
| No clear differentiation | Hard to establish authority | Find unique angle |
| No first-hand experience | E-E-A-T weakness | Build experience signals |
